​JUSTIFICATION OF THE SYSTEM --------------------------- ​This tuning system breaks with the artificial uniformity of modern equal temperament. By utilizing frequency ratios that closely approximate the Golden Ratio (\phi) and the Pure Harmonic Series, it achieves maximum harmonic resonance. ​Particularly in the C-Major form—and when using a capo on the 2nd, 3rd, or 4th frets—the overtones lock in mathematically. The result is a sustained, bell-like tone, devoid of the "nervous" beat frequencies found in standard tuning. This is a system for those who seek the mathematical order of nature within sound. Note: Tune using the natural harmonics at the 5th and 7th frets, for example.
